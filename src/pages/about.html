Our objectives

  • Maintain daily worship and ritual at every temple under the Trust.
  • Preserve and repair temple structures, idols, and ritual articles.
  • Celebrate festivals in accordance with tradition, open to all devotees.
  • Run Annadanam, education, and medical relief for the surrounding community.
  • Administer all offerings transparently and apply them only to Trust purposes.

Governance

The Trust is managed by a board of trustees who serve voluntarily. Accounts are audited annually and are available for inspection at the Trust office on request.
